# Project specification

## Product

A fast static marketing site for **ARBISOFT** at `arbisoft.biz`, targeting founders and operating teams that need practical AI, automation, and digital-business help.

## Approved services

1. AI Solutions & Development
2. AI Automation & Technical Support
3. E-commerce & Digital Business Consulting
4. AI & Digital Strategy Sessions

Trading, crypto services, hosted checkout, customer accounts, dashboards, databases, and an in-site payment processor are outside V1.

## Primary journey

Every commercial path should lead to a prefilled WhatsApp conversation. After a genuine scope and price agreement, the owner creates the client and invoice/payment request in Bonsai and sends its link. Stripe can be connected in Bonsai for card payments where available. Wise, Payoneer, IBAN, and bank transfer are recorded as external/offline methods where Bonsai and the owner's accounts support them.

## Pages and content

The static experience must include Home, Services, Process, About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, and Refund Policy content. A compact one-page structure with anchored navigation is acceptable when it improves speed and coherence. Include a real 404 page, metadata, favicon, accessible navigation, reduced-motion support, and clear non-JavaScript access to core content.
