# Owner operations

## Before launch

- Brand, domain, and WhatsApp are approved as ARBISOFT, `arbisoft.biz`, and `+212 661 487 396`.
- Approve the contact email, legal identity, jurisdiction, address, tax details, and final policies.
- Complete the Zoho Invoice test workflow and verify fees/method availability.
- Have legal and tax professionals review the commercial documents appropriate to the business.

## Lead handling

1. Acknowledge the inquiry and ask for the current situation, desired outcome, timing, and decision maker.
2. Do not quote before enough scope is known.
3. Summarize the agreed scope and explicitly list exclusions.
4. Move formal scope, contract, and invoicing into Zoho Invoice.
5. Confirm payment from the provider/Zoho Invoice record, not a customer screenshot.

## Delivery record

Keep a private, access-controlled record of the agreement, milestones, changes, approvals, invoice, cleared payment, handoff, and completion. Store no client records in this public website repository.

## Site maintenance

- Review services, policy drafts, external links, Zoho Invoice steps, and fee references quarterly.
- Preview changes on a non-production branch before merging.
- Avoid adding trackers, forms, or third-party embeds without a privacy review.
- Keep the site static unless a verified business requirement justifies additional infrastructure.
