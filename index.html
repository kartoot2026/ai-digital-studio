02 / Our process

From first conversation
to a documented delivery.

We keep the process simple and transparent, with clear steps from the initial discussion to the final handover.

  1. 01

    Discuss

    Share the business context, constraint, and desired outcome on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    Define

    We agree on scope, deliverables, timeline, price, and responsibilities on both sides.

  3. 03

    Confirm

    You receive a Zoho Invoice quote, invoice, or secure payment request after the agreement—not before.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Work is completed against the agreed scope, with a clear handoff and recorded completion.

The website does not collect payments. Payment options are provided through the agreed invoice workflow where available.
