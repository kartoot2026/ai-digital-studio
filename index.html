02 / Process

From first conversation to a documented delivery.

  1. 01

    Discuss

    Share the business context, constraint, and desired outcome on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    Define

    We agree on scope, deliverables, timeline, price, and the responsibilities on both sides.

  3. 03

    Confirm

    You receive a Bonsai invoice or payment request after the agreement—not before.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Work is completed against the agreed scope, with a clear handoff and recorded completion.

The website does not collect payments. Card and external transfer options are provided through the agreed invoice workflow where available.
