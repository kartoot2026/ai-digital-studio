Refund Policy

Owner review required: the applicable project agreement and mandatory local consumer rights take priority. This page is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Before work starts

Any cancellation or refund right is stated in the agreed proposal, contract, or invoice. Contact ARBISOFT promptly if you believe a payment was made in error.

Work already performed

Fees relating to completed milestones, delivered work, reserved time, or non-recoverable costs may not be refundable where permitted by law and agreed in writing.

How requests are reviewed

Each request is reviewed against the written project agreement, completed work, payment record, and applicable law. Approved refunds are returned through an appropriate available method and may require processing time from the payment provider.

Raise a concern

Email support@arbisoft.biz or use the contact page. Include the invoice reference, payment date, and a concise explanation. Do not send full card or banking credentials.
